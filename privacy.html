Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

This policy applies to RescueGrid Responder and the RescueGrid operational platform.

Information we process

Depending on organizational configuration and the features you use, RescueGrid may process your name, phone number, address, blood group, emergency-contact information, team and deployment assignment, responder profile photo, mission status, safety status, SOS events, mission notes and messages, device/synchronization information, uploaded field media, and precise location information.

Location information

RescueGrid Responder uses precise location while you use the responder app for mission coordination, operational positioning, mission check-ins, safety confirmations, SOS events, and supported offline synchronization. The current Android release does not request Android background-location permission. Location is not collected or used for advertising.

Camera and notifications

Camera access is used only when you choose to capture or upload a responder profile or mission image. If you enable notifications, they may be used for operational messages, mission information, and responder-safety reminders.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to coordinate emergency operations, verify responder access, assign and manage missions, display operational status and location, deliver messages and safety reminders, support SAFE check-ins and SOS events, synchronize supported offline activity, and maintain incident records. Operational information may be shared with authorized personnel of the participating organization or response structure that manages your team. RescueGrid does not sell personal or precise-location data.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the operational nature of the service, including authenticated device access and controlled organizational access. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Responder account/profile data is retained while the account is active and as reasonably necessary to operate the service. You may request account deletion through the in-app “Delete Account / Data” link or at this account deletion page. When deletion is completed, direct profile/contact data is removed or anonymized. De-identified operational records may be retained where reasonably necessary for incident documentation, responder safety, fraud/security prevention, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

Children

RescueGrid Responder is intended for authorized responders and organizational personnel. It is not designed as a children-directed app.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

RescueGrid provides emergency response, rescue coordination, responder communication, location tracking, safety monitoring and mission management services.

Information we process

Depending on organizational configuration, RescueGrid may process identity and contact information, team assignment, responder profile information, mission assignments, operational and safety status, messages and alerts, uploaded field media, device and synchronization information, and precise location information.

Location information

When location-dependent functions are enabled, RescueGrid may collect precise location. During an authorized active mission, location may continue in the background so authorized Command personnel can monitor responder position and safety.

How information is used

Information is used to coordinate emergency operations, assign missions, display responder locations, support responder safety, deliver operational messages and alerts, support SAFE check-ins and SOS events, enable team communications, synchronize offline activity and maintain operational records.

Organization access

Authorized personnel of participating organizations may access operational information necessary to manage responders and missions.

Permissions

RescueGrid may request location and background-location access, notifications, microphone access for Push-to-Talk, and camera/photo access for approved field documentation and profile images.

Security and retention

Reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. Operational data may be retained as reasonably necessary for incident records, safety, organizational requirements, legal obligations and system security.

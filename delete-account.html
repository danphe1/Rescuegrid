Delete RescueGrid Account

Use this page to delete a RescueGrid Responder account. You will need the phone number and rescuer code used to sign in.

Before deleting

If a mission is currently active, end the mission first. Account deletion is permanent and will sign the responder out.

Verify your account

What is deleted

Your direct profile/contact information, login credential, device token, profile photo and direct notification subscription are removed or anonymized. Direct mission-photo records linked to the account are removed.

What may be retained

De-identified operational records may be retained where reasonably necessary for incident documentation, responder safety, fraud/security prevention, dispute resolution or legal obligations. These retained records are no longer intended to identify you as an active RescueGrid account holder.

Need help?

If you cannot use this form, contact privacy@rescuegrid.app.

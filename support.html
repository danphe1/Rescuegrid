RescueGrid Support

Help with RescueGrid Responder, mission access, notifications, GPS, safety check-ins, SOS, messaging and account issues.

I cannot log in

Confirm your registered telephone number and rescuer code, and make sure your device/account has been approved by your organization.

My location is not updating

Enable Location and Precise Location when prompted and keep the responder app open while location tracking is needed. The current Android release does not request Android background-location permission.

I am not receiving safety notifications

Enable RescueGrid notifications when prompted and check your phone notification settings. Notification availability can depend on browser/TWA support and your organization’s configuration.

I have no mobile data

Supported GPS points may be queued on the device and synchronized when connectivity returns. Phone/browser suspension can still stop new GPS capture.

Delete my account

Use the in-app “Delete Account / Data” link or the account deletion page.

Emergency notice

RescueGrid is an operational coordination tool and does not replace local emergency telephone numbers, radio networks or established emergency procedures.

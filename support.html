RescueGrid Support

Help with RescueGrid Responder, mission access, notifications, GPS, Push-to-Talk and account issues.

I cannot log in

Confirm your registered telephone number, responder code, and that your device has been approved by your organization.

My location is not updating

Enable Location, Precise Location and background access. Make sure battery optimization is not restricting RescueGrid.

I am not receiving Command notifications

Enable RescueGrid notifications in the app and in your phone settings.

Push-to-Talk is not working

Confirm microphone permission, an available network connection, and the correct Team or Command channel.

I have no mobile data

Supported field activity may be queued locally and synchronized when connectivity returns. Voice/PTT requires network access unless another communication method is configured.

Emergency notice

RescueGrid is an operational coordination tool and does not replace local emergency telephone numbers, radio networks or established emergency procedures.
